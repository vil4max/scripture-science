# Homepage reading structure

Owner: Codex. Scope: the owner's 21 homepage browser comments, 2026-09-26.

## Outcome

Merge the family introduction and eight profile cards into one section. Group
Christian traditions visually, separating traditional Trinitarian doctrine from
Latter-day Saint and Jehovah's Witness teachings without assigning a universal
judgment about Christian identity. Keep Adventists in the Trinitarian group:
their official current beliefs explicitly affirm the Trinity.

Remove the duplicated 2020 table, methodological captions, repeated evidence
footer and homepage legal marker. Preserve the table, methodology, legal context,
photo credits and licenses in Sources. Clicking a photograph reaches its credit.
Retain the chart's year, approximate labels and interactive reading instructions.
Use short descriptive headings for other religions.

## Acceptance

All eight profiles occur once, with comparison actions preserved. No global
selector returns. The map and single diagram remain readable at desktop width.
All new claim evidence is discoverable in Sources; image credits retain authors,
licenses, original links and modification statements. The homepage contains no
legal star, repeated source claim or methodology disclosures.

## Decisions

Family grouping supersedes global age order on the homepage; order within each
subgroup remains historical. This is a doctrinal grouping, not a blanket claim
that a tradition is or is not Christian. The owner's request to move noise into
Sources supersedes the old homepage footnote and inline photo-credit placement.
Other reading pages retain their existing legal-note behavior.

Validation: `npm run verify` passed (92 tests, Astro check and text fidelity).
Desktop browser inspection confirmed eight cards, no horizontal overflow and
photo navigation to the matching Sources credit. New official belief pages and
Harvard Pluralism Project introductions were opened; existing doctrinal evidence
was reused from the matrix. Full remote source re-fetch was not run.
