Session date: 2026-09-25.

Scope: Protestantism as one comparison-topic tradition covering seven families —
Lutheran, Reformed/Presbyterian, Anglican, Baptist (including the Russian Union
of Evangelical Christians-Baptists, РС ЕХБ), Methodist, Pentecostal/Charismatic,
and non-denominational evangelicals — one section per the site's 8-point

- **[official]** — the tradition's own body, official site, or official text

## Protestantism (Протестантизм)

### 0. What the family shares

All seven families trace themselves to the 16th-century European Reformation
and its Radical/Free-Church and later (Wesleyan, Pentecostal) offshoots, and
converge on the Reformation's own summary of its distinctives — the "five
solas." A Russian academic source states this explicitly:

- "Логика развития протестантизма была заложена в тезисах Реформации Quinque
  sola (Пять «только»): только верой, только писанием, только благодатью,
  только Христос, только Богу слава." **[reference]** Roman Lunkin (Институт
  Европы РАН), ["Российский протестантизм: евангельские христиане как новый
  социальный феномен"](http://www.sov-europe.ru/2014/3/Lun1.pdf), *Современная
  Европа* 3/2014, p. 134.

Shared across every family verified below: a 66-book biblical canon excluding
the Apocrypha (§2), justification by grace through faith rather than by works
or church ritual (§5 "Salvation"), the priesthood of all believers, and the
rejection of papal jurisdiction. Where families differ sharply — the mode and
subjects of baptism, the nature of Christ's presence in the Lord's Supper,
church government, predestination vs. free will, and the Pentecostal/charismatic
doctrine of Spirit-baptism — each point is flagged explicitly in the sections
below rather than papered over.

### 1. Identity and self-understanding

**Shared framing.** Protestant bodies generally describe the Reformation not as
founding a new church but as restoring or purifying the existing one. The
clearest statement of this self-understanding found this session is Lutheran:

- "Лютеране подчеркивают непрерывность развития христианства и убеждены, что в
  лютеранской Реформации не создана новая церковь, а восстановлена древняя."
  **[official]** [ELCROM, "Лютеранство"](https://www.elkras.ru/vo_chto_veryat_liuterane/liuteranstvo.jdx).

### 2. Scripture

**Canon.** All families verified this session hold the same 66-book Protestant
canon (39 Old Testament + 27 New Testament) and exclude the Apocrypha:

- РС ЕХБ: "состоит из 39 книг Ветхого Завета и 27 книг Нового Завета."
  **[official]** [РС ЕХБ, Вероучение](https://baptist.org.ru/o-baptistah/baptistskoe-verouchenie/).
- РЦХВЕ: "данное нам в 66 канонических книгах Ветхого и Нового Заветов –
  дословно богодухновенное." **[official]** [РЦХВЕ, Confession of Faith](https://rchve.ru/o-nas/verouchenie/3841-ispovedanie-very-rts-khve).
- Westminster Confession ch. 1 (Reformed standard, in current use by a Russian
  congregation, see §3): "Книги, именуемые Апокрифами, не являются
  богодухновенными и не входят в канон Священного Писания." **[official]**
  [ireformed.org, Westminster Confession ch. 1](https://www.ireformed.org/westminster-confession-of-faith).

### 5. Positions on the comparison topics

Each topic gives the shared Protestant position first (where one exists), then
family-specific differences with sources.

**God (oneness, Trinity, nature).** Shared: one God in three co-equal,
co-eternal persons (Nicene Trinitarianism), against both Arianism and
modalism.
- Lutheran (Augsburg Conf. Art. I): "существует одно Божественное Существо,
  именуемое Богом; ...в этом Едином Божественном Существе суть три Ипостаси
  (три Лица)." **[official]** [ELCROM](https://www.elkras.ru/vo_chto_veryat_liuterane/verouchitelnye_knigi/kniga_soglasiya/augsburgskoe_veroispovedanie.jdx).
- Baptist (РС ЕХБ): "Мы веруем в триединого, единосущного Бога Отца, Сына и
  Святого Духа." **[official]** [baptist.org.ru](https://baptist.org.ru/o-baptistah/baptistskoe-verouchenie/).
- Pentecostal (РЦХВЕ): "один Бог, особым образом вечно существующий в Трех
  Лицах – Отец, Сын и Святой Дух." **[official]** [rchve.ru](https://rchve.ru/o-nas/verouchenie/3841-ispovedanie-very-rts-khve).
- Anglican (39 Articles, Art. I, Russian academic translation): "Есть только
  один живой и истинный Бог, присносущный, бесплотный, нераздельный,
  бесстрастный, обладающий бесконечными силой, премудростью и благостию."
  **[reference/academic]** [em-england.ru](https://em-england.ru/articles/culture/c05).
- Reformed: Westminster Confession devotes ch. 2 to "О Боге - Святой Троице"
  (chapter title confirmed; body text not captured this session — `TODO: verify`).
  **[official]** [ireformed.org](https://www.ireformed.org/westminster-confession-of-faith).
- Methodist: "Верую в Единого Бога," with the page emphasizing that God's power
  is revealed through Christ's death and resurrection. **[official]** [UMC
  Eurasia, "Верить"](https://umc-eurasia.ru/eurasia.umcchurches.org/верить/).

**The name of God.** No family in this file has a distinctive "sacred name"
doctrine. Every translation checked (Synodal, NRT) renders the Tetragrammaton
as "Господь" (LORD) rather than a transliterated proper name (§2) — a direct,
citable contrast with Jehovah's Witnesses' "Иегова," useful for this site's
comparison table, stated here neutrally as a translation-convention fact rather
than an evaluation.

**Jesus Christ.** Shared: fully God and fully man, virgin birth, atoning death,
bodily resurrection, ascension, return as judge.
- Lutheran (Augsburg Art. III): "Един Христос, истинный Бог и истинный
  человек, истинно рожденный, страдавший, распятый, умерший и погребенный...
  и воистину воскрес в третий день из мертвых." **[official]** ELCROM, above.
- Pentecostal (РЦХВЕ): Christ "принял плоть от Духа Святого и девы Марии...
  телесно воскрес из мертвых для оправдания нашего, вознесся на небеса и
  воссел одесную Отца." **[official]** rchve.ru, above.
- Baptist (РС ЕХБ): "Иисус Христос есть Сын Божий, от вечности сущий Бог,"
  "единый посредник между Богом и человеком." **[official]** baptist.org.ru,
  above.
- Methodist: "Иисус Христос – это Бог-Сын, который, как человек, родился
  среди нас," affirming virgin birth and resurrection. **[official]** UMC
  Eurasia, "Верить," above.

**The Holy Spirit — the clearest doctrinal fault line inside the family.**
Shared: full deity of the Spirit, active at Pentecost. Distinctive:
Pentecostal/Charismatic teaching of a second, subsequent "baptism in the Holy
Spirit," evidenced by tongues, which the other families do not teach in the
same terms.
- Pentecostal (РЦХВЕ): "физическим доказательством принятия крещения Духом
  Святым является знамение говорения на иных языках," distinct from the
  experience of new birth ("опыт крещения Духом Святым отличается от опыта
  возрождения свыше"). **[official]** rchve.ru, above.
- Baptist (РС ЕХБ), by contrast, describes the Spirit's Pentecost role
  (empowerment, conviction, church-building) with no tongues-as-evidence
  doctrine: "Дух Святой был ниспослан в день Пятидесятницы для свидетельства о
  Христе, для прославления Его и для созидания Церкви." **[official]**
  baptist.org.ru, above.

**Scripture and canon.** Covered in full in §2 (shared 66-book canon, sola
scriptura, Apocrypha excluded).

**Tradition, and who interprets — a second major fault line.**
- Confessional families (Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican) hold a subordinate but
  real role for creeds/confessions as guides to reading Scripture (§3),
  explicitly ranked below Scripture itself: the Lutheran Formula of Concord
  calls its own confessions "просто свидетельства и изложения веры... каким
  образом в различные времена понималось и истолковывалось Священное
  Писание," while Scripture alone is "единственный судья, правило и норма."
  **[official]** [ELCROM, "Лютеранство"](https://www.elkras.ru/vo_chto_veryat_liuterane/liuteranstvo.jdx).
- Baptist and Pentecostal families explicitly withhold binding, creed-like
  status from their own confessions: the 1985 ЕХБ Вероучение was adopted
  "в качестве вспомогательного материала для духовного воспитания верующих"
  (auxiliary material for believers' spiritual education), not a test of
  fellowship. **[official]** [baptist-volga.ru](https://baptist-volga.ru/verouchenie-baptistov).
  Interpretive authority in these families sits with the local congregation.

**Salvation.** Shared: salvation is by grace through faith, not by works or
sacrament, grounded in Christ's substitutionary death.
- Lutheran (Augsburg Art. IV): "мы получаем прощение грехов и оправдание перед
  Богом ради Христа по благодати через веру." **[official]** ELCROM, above.
- Pentecostal (РЦХВЕ): salvation is "от Бога по благодати," received "посредством
  покаяния пред Богом и веры в Евангелие," with "возрождение Духом Святым"
  necessary. **[official]** rchve.ru, above.
- Divergence on predestination vs. free will: the Westminster Confession (Reformed)
  devotes ch. 3 to "О вечных Божьих установлениях" (of God's eternal decrees)
  and ch. 17 to "О стойкости святых" (perseverance of the saints) — chapter
  titles confirming classical Calvinist predestination/eternal-security
  doctrine is part of the Reformed standard. **[official]** [ireformed.org](https://www.ireformed.org/westminster-confession-of-faith).
  `TODO: verify` — chapter body text for ch. 3/17 was not captured this
  session, and no equivalent explicit statement (for or against predestination)
  was found in the Baptist, Pentecostal or Methodist confessions checked —
  their texts are simply silent on the Calvinist/Arminian question at the
  level of detail quoted here, so no claim is made about where they fall.

**Soul, death, afterlife, and judgement.** Shared: bodily resurrection and a
final judgement; no family checked this session mentions purgatory.
- Baptist (РС ЕХБ): "воскрешение всех умерших и... праведный суд Божий перед
  Великим белым престолом." **[official]** baptist.org.ru, above.
- Lutheran: belief affirmed in "Страшный суд и жизнь после смерти" alongside
  the Trinity and Christ's two natures. **[official]** ELCROM, "Лютеранство,"
  above.
- Reformed: Westminster ch. 32 "О состоянии человека после смерти и о
  воскрешении из мертвых" and ch. 33 "О последнем суде" (titles confirmed;
  body text not captured — `TODO: verify`). **[official]** ireformed.org, above.

**End times and the future of the world.** Explicit, near-identical
premillennial/rapture language appears in both the Baptist and Pentecostal
confessions checked — a notable cross-family parallel not shared by the older
(16th–17th-century) confessions, which predate this framework and do not use
it.
- Baptist (РС ЕХБ): "...невидимое для мира восхищение Церкви и встреча ее со
  Христом в облаках," preceding "второе пришествие... в силе и славе."
  **[official]** baptist.org.ru, above.
- Pentecostal (РЦХВЕ) lists as goals of the second coming: "- восхищение
  Церкви; - спасение остатка еврейской нации; - поражение антихриста... -
  установление Своего тысячелетнего Царствия на земле." **[official]**
  rchve.ru, above.
- Lutheran and Reformed confessions (1530/1640s) affirm Christ's visible
  return and final judgement (Augsburg Art. XVII "О втором пришествии Христа и
  о суде"; Westminster ch. 33) without rapture/millennium language — `TODO:
  verify` full body text of both chapters was not captured this session, so
  this is stated as an absence-of-terminology observation, not a positive
  doctrinal claim about their eschatology's structure.

**Holy days, sabbath, festivals.** Baptist doctrine gives an explicit
theological argument for Sunday, not Saturday, as the day of worship — useful
for this site's comparison with Sabbatarian traditions:
- РС ЕХБ: "первый день недели был днем, в который Иисус Христос воскрес для
  оправдания нашего," also the day of Pentecost and of Christ's post-resurrection
  appearances; "Воскресный день... должен быть днем святого труда для
  Господа... изучению Слова Божия, пребыванию в молитве." **[official]**
  baptist.org.ru, "О воскресном дне."
- Anglican and Lutheran retain a liturgical calendar and set forms: St
  Andrew's, Moscow still holds "традиционная служба 1662 года... по
  воскресеньям в 9 часов утра" alongside a modern-language service.
  **[official]** moscowanglican.org, above.

**Organisation and clergy.** A third clear fault line, from episcopal to
congregational polity:
- Episcopal: Anglican (historic threefold order, "тройственные чины церкви —
  диаконов, священников и епископов," per moscowanglican.org's "Англиканство"
  page) and Methodist (bishops; Rüdiger Minor was "bishop of the Eurasia
  Area" from 1991, per UMC Eurasia, above).
- Presbyterian/synodical: Reformed — Westminster ch. 31 "О Синодах и Соборах"
  (title confirmed). **[official]** ireformed.org, above.
- Congregational: Baptist — pastors/elders are "поставля[ются]... через
  призвание и избрание церковью" (called and elected by the local
  congregation). **[official]** baptist.org.ru, above.
- Pentecostal unions mix a "начальствующий епископ" (presiding bishop) title
  at the union level — confirmed for РЦХВЕ via its own site — with
  congregational-level autonomy. **[official]** [rchve.ru](https://rchve.ru/o-nas/verouchenie/3841-ispovedanie-very-rts-khve)
  (news item referencing "начальствующий епископ РЦХВЕ").
- Women's ordination varies by family, not by a single Protestant rule: "многим
  это не мешает, например, принимать женское священство (в методизме,
  лютеранстве, пятидесятничестве)," while Baptists are noted in the same
  source as generally more conservative on this point. **[reference]** Lunkin
  (2014), p. 135.

**Distinctive rules (diet, blood, military, state).** Thinly and unevenly
sourced this session — presented only where directly evidenced, not
generalized across the family:
- РС ЕХБ affirms ordinary civic obedience with no explicit pacifism: "Христианин
  должен быть примерным гражданином своего отечества, он призван повиноваться
  власти." **[official]** baptist.org.ru, "Отношение к государству."
- No dietary or blood-transfusion restriction is mentioned in any confession
  text read this session (Lutheran, Reformed ch. 1/29, Anglican Art. I/VI/XXV/XXVII/XXVIII,
  Baptist, Pentecostal) — noted as an absence, not asserted as a universal
  Protestant rule, since most of these documents simply do not address diet at

### 6. Sensitive points and common misconceptions to avoid

- **"Protestant" is not one thing.** The doctrinal distance between, say,
  Lutheran sacramental theology and Baptist/Pentecostal memorialism (§5) is as
  large as the distance between some other traditions this site compares —
  avoid treating "Protestants believe X" as a safe generalization; this file
  flags each point where the families diverge.
- **"Евангельские христиане" (Russian self-designation) vs. English
  "evangelical."** Russian Baptists, Pentecostals and others commonly call
  themselves "евангельские христиане" or simply "христиане" (not Orthodox,
  not Catholic) — a theological/historical label distinct from the American
  political sense of "evangelical." Lunkin's academic framing: "Протестанты
  разных направлений называют себя евангельскими христианами либо просто
  христианами." **[reference]** Lunkin (2014), p. 133.
- **Jehovah's Witnesses and Latter-day Saints are not part of this family.**
  They are covered separately on this site (`restorationist-adventist-lds-jw.md`)
  and should not be folded into "Protestantism" — they reject core items this
  file documents as shared across all seven Protestant families (e.g. Nicene
  Trinitarianism, §5 "God").
- **Anglican "via media" is not simply "Catholic-lite."** Its own official
  description is a deliberate middle position holding Reformation sola-scriptura
  commitments together with retained pre-Reformation order and worship
  (§1, §3) — not a watered-down Catholicism nor a variant Baptist-style
  low-church Protestantism.
- **Editorial rule applied throughout this file:** no evaluative language
  ("секта," "лжеучение," etc.) is used for any family; where a critique from
  another tradition might be relevant, it is not included in this research
  file at all (none was sourced this session) and should only ever appear on
  a separate, sourced "How others critique it" line per the site's rules.
