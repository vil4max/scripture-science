## Shared historical background: the Second Great Awakening, Millerism, and restorationism

All three movements below trace their origin to the religious ferment of the early-to-mid-19th-century United States, not to one another — SDA and the Bible Student movement (→ Jehovah's Witnesses) both grew directly out of the Millerite Adventist movement's aftermath, while the Latter-day Saint movement arose independently, a few years earlier, from the same general revivalist environment.

**The Second Great Awakening** (broadly the 1790s–1840s) was a wave of Protestant revivalism that reshaped American religious life through camp meetings and "protracted meetings."

- "In the early nineteenth century, a succession of religious revivals collectively known as the Second Great Awakening remade the nation's religious landscape." **[reference]** [The American Yawp, ch. 10 "Religion and Reform"](https://www.americanyawp.com/text/10-religion-and-reform/).
- "By the 1820s evangelicalism had become one of the most dynamic and important cultural forces in American life." **[reference]** [National Humanities Center, "Evangelicalism, Revivalism, and the Second Great Awakening"](https://nationalhumanitiescenter.org/tserve/nineteen/nkeyinfo/nevanrev.htm).

**Restorationism (primitivism)** — the drive to strip away centuries of tradition and "restore" original New Testament Christianity — was a defining impulse of the period, expressed differently by different movements:

- "Some, like Alexander Campbell and Barton Stone, proposed a return to (or 'restoration' of) New Testament Christianity, stripped of centuries of additional teachings and practices." **[reference]** [The American Yawp, ch. 10](https://www.americanyawp.com/text/10-religion-and-reform/).
- "Other restorationists built on the foundation laid by the evangelical churches by using their methods and means to both critique the Protestant mainstream and move beyond the accepted boundaries of contemporary Christian orthodoxy." **[reference]** same source. All three traditions in this file describe their own origin in restorationist terms (each tradition's own wording is quoted in its own §1, not repeated here).

**Millerism.** William Miller, a Baptist preacher, concluded from his study of biblical prophecy (chiefly Daniel) that Christ's Second Advent would occur around 1843–1844; the movement he inspired grew to tens of thousands of adherents by 1843–44.

- "Miller was a one-time 'deist' and grandson of a Baptist minister whose study of the Bible led him to conclude that the second coming would take place between 1843 and 1844." **[reference]** [Jeri Wieringa, "The Emergence of Seventh-day Adventism" (dissertation), ch. 1](https://dissertation.jeriwieringa.com/essays/chapter-1/).
- "By 1843, Miller had garnered a following of over fifty thousand adherents, known as Millerites." **[reference]** [EBSCO Research Starters, "Millerism"](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/history/millerism).
- A specific date, October 22, 1844, was reached after an earlier date (March 21, 1844) passed: "Samuel Snow announced that he had identified the error in Miller's calculations and that October 22, 1844, was the date for the second coming." **[reference]** [Wieringa dissertation, ch. 1](https://dissertation.jeriwieringa.com/essays/chapter-1/).
- "When October 22 came and went without the promised apocalypse, many of Miller's followers became disillusioned. They referred to the event as the 'Great Disappointment.'" **[reference]** [EBSCO Research Starters, "Millerism"](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/history/millerism).

**How each of the three movements in this file connects to that background** (detail in each tradition's own §1):

- **Seventh-day Adventist Church**: direct institutional descendant of the Millerite movement's aftermath among Adventists who kept their faith after 1844 (see SDA §1 for the church's own account and the 1863 organization date).
- **Jehovah's Witnesses**: Charles Taze Russell's formative religious experience was hearing a Second Adventist preacher, Jonas Wendell, in 1870 — an explicitly Adventist-milieu origin for what became the Bible Student movement. "There, for the first time, I heard something of the views of Second Adventists, the preacher being Mr. Jonas Wendell." **[official]** [Watchtower Online Library, *Jehovah's Witnesses — Proclaimers of God's Kingdom*, "Part 1—Early Voices (1870-1878)"](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1955001) (see JW §1 for the fuller chain of dates).
- **The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints**: arose independently and slightly earlier (Joseph Smith's First Vision, traditionally dated 1820; church organized 1830), from the same general climate of revivalism and restorationist yearning in the northeastern United States, but is not a Millerite/Adventist offshoot — it has its own separate founding narrative (see LDS §1). `TODO: verify` — the specific historiographic term "burned-over district" for the western-New-York revival intensity in which Smith grew up is well attested in the general literature, but was not confirmed against a source directly opened in this session (Britannica blocked; web-search budget was exhausted before an alternative was located), so it is not asserted above as a quoted/sourced term.

## Seventh-day Adventist Church (Церковь христиан адвентистов седьмого дня)

*Note on the church's own name:* every officially-registered SDA site consulted this session uses the fuller form **"Церковь христиан адвентистов седьмого дня"**, not the shorter "Церковь адвентистов седьмого дня." **[official]** [adventist.ru](https://adventist.ru/) — page title "Церкви Христиан-Адвентистов Седьмого Дня"; **[official]** [mosadvent.ru](https://www.mosadvent.ru/index.php/adout/faith/item/509-polnoe-izlozhenie-doktrin) — footer "Централизованная религиозная организация Московское объединение Церкви Христиан Адвентистов Седьмого Дня."

### 1. Identity and self-understanding

**Founding.** The church traces its roots to the transdenominational American Millerite movement of the early 1840s (Baptist preacher William Miller's reading of Daniel 8:14; see the shared historical background above), the "Great Disappointment" of **October 22, 1844**, and the formal organization of the **General Conference of Seventh-day Adventists in May 1863**. Faith-community and scholarly dating agree here; no discrepancy was found.

- **[official]** [Ellen G. White Estate, biography](https://whiteestate.org/about/egwbio/) — "The keenness of the Great Disappointment that Jesus did not return to earth on October 22, 1844." / "The climax was reached in the organization of the General Conference of Seventh-day Adventists in May, 1863."
- **[reference]** [World Council of Churches, "Seventh-day Adventist Church"](https://www.oikoumene.org/church-families/seventh-day-adventist-church) — "The church arose out of the eschatological expectations of the middle nineteenth century... but was only formally organized in 1863." / "The Millerites had set October 22, 1844, for the return of Christ. With the failure of this date, the movement fell into disarray."
- **[official]** E. V. Zaytsev, *"На основе Божьего Слова. Краткая история доктринального становления Церкви адвентистов седьмого дня"* (Заокский: Источник жизни, 2019), published by the Euro-Asia Division's own Biblical Research Institute — [PDF](https://bri.esd.adventist.org/uploaded_assets/100451) — "движение У. Миллера (1782—1849)... сыграло ключевую роль в появлении Церкви адвентистов седьмого дня."

**Self-understanding.** The church presents itself as recovering biblical teaching, not issuing a new revelation replacing the Bible — a "remnant" people continuing, not superseding, historic Christianity.

- **[official]** [mosadvent.ru, "Полное изложение доктрин"](https://www.mosadvent.ru/index.php/adout/faith/item/509-polnoe-izlozhenie-doktrin), Belief 13 ("Церковь Остатка и её миссия") — "Вселенская Церковь состоит из тех, кто истинно верит в Христа. Но в последние дни... Бог призвал Церковь Остатка к соблюдению заповедей Божьих и сохранению веры в Иисуса" (Откр. 12:17; 14:6–12).
- **[official]** [mosadvent.ru, distinctive-doctrines essay](https://www.mosadvent.ru/index.php/adout/faith/item/510-chto-otlichaet-adventistskoe-verouchenie) — "Адвентистская церковь была основана как движение, как нечто открытое для нового, для развития и продвижения вперед."

### 2. Scripture

**Canon.** Belief 1 describes Scripture as "Ветхий и Новый Завет" without stating a book-count in the text found; the standard 66-book Protestant figure was not located in any SDA source opened this session (`TODO: verify`).

- **[official]** [mosadvent.ru](https://www.mosadvent.ru/index.php/adout/faith/item/509-polnoe-izlozhenie-doktrin), Belief 1 — "Священное Писание, состоящее из Ветхого и Нового Заветов, есть Слово Божье, письменно переданное по Божественному вдохновению через святых мужей Божьих... Библия — это непогрешимое откровение Его воли" (2 Петр. 1:20,21; 2 Тим. 3:16,17).

**Russian translation used.** Two Biblical Research Institute (Euro-Asia Division) books state explicitly that Bible quotations follow the **Синодальный перевод** (Synodal Translation), with the modern Institute-for-Bible-Translation (Zaoksky, "ИПБ") rendering used only where separately marked.

- **[official]** [BRI/ESD, *"Полноценная жизнь: адвентистский взгляд на здоровье"* (2025), PDF](https://bri.esd.adventist.org/uploaded_assets/114816) — "Цитаты из Библии в данной книге приводятся по Синодальному переводу, за исключением тех случаев, когда... «ИПБ»."
- **[official]** [BRI/ESD, *"Библия и церковь о проблеме войны и мира"* (2023), PDF](https://bri.esd.adventist.org/uploaded_assets/113416) — "Все библейские цитаты в данном сборнике приводятся по Синодальному переводу Библии."

**Online verse example (John 3:16, Synodal):**

> **Ин. 3:16** — «Ибо так возлюбил Бог мир, что отдал Сына Своего Единородного, дабы всякий, верующий в Него, не погиб, но имел жизнь вечную».

- [bible.by/verse/43/3/16/](https://bible.by/verse/43/3/16/) — page labels the text "Синодальный перевод" and gives this verse verbatim as quoted.

### 5. Positions on the comparison topics

All quotations below are from the 28 Fundamental Beliefs, **[official]** [mosadvent.ru](https://www.mosadvent.ru/index.php/adout/faith/item/509-polnoe-izlozhenie-doktrin), Synodal-translation versification, unless marked otherwise.

- **God (Trinity).** Belief 2, *Троица*: "Бог един. Отец, Сын и Святой Дух — единство трёх совечных Личностей. Бог бессмертен, всемогущ, всеведущ, превыше всего и вездесущ" (Втор. 6:4; Мф. 28:19; 2 Кор. 13:13).
- **The name of God.** No distinctive "sacred name" teaching was found; Beliefs 2–5 consistently use "Бог," "Господь," "Отец, Сын и Святой Дух" without singling out a specific name-form as doctrinally required. `TODO: verify` there is no separate official statement elsewhere.
- **Jesus (Messiah).** Belief 4, *Бог Сын*: "Бог Вечный Сын явился воплощенным в Иисусе Христе... Он был действительно Богом — обещанным Мессией. Он добровольно пострадал и умер на кресте за наши грехи... Он опять придёт в этот мир во славе" (Ин. 1:1—3,14; Рим. 6:23).
- **The Holy Spirit.** Belief 5: "Бог Вечный Дух Святой действовал вместе с Отцом и Сыном при сотворении, воплощении и искуплении... Он наделяет Церковь духовными дарами... и наставляет её на всякую истину" (Деян. 1:8; Ин. 16:7—13).
- **Scripture and canon.** Belief 1, quoted in full in §2.
- **Tradition/interpretive authority (Ellen White).** Belief 18, quoted in full in §3; the church states her writings are explicitly not equal to Scripture (whiteestate.org quotes, §3).
- **Salvation.** Belief 10, *Спасение во Христе*: "Христос стал грехом ради нас, чтобы в Нем мы соделались праведными перед Богом... Вера, посредством которой мы принимаем спасение, приходит к нам от Божественной силы Его Слова и является даром Божьей благодати" (2 Кор. 5:17—21; Еф. 2:5—10).
- **Soul, death, afterlife, judgement.** Belief 26, *Смерть и воскресение*: "Возмездие за грех — смерть. Но Бог, единственный имеющий бессмертие, дарует вечную жизнь Своим искупленным. До дня Второго пришествия смерть для всех людей — это состояние небытия" (Рим. 6:23; Еккл. 9:5,6). Belief 24 names the pre-Advent judgement explicitly: "В 1844 году, по окончании пророческого периода в 2300 лет... на небе начался следственный суд («investigative judgement»)— первая стадия окончательного удаления всех грехов" (Дан. 7:9—27; 8:13,14). Belief 27: the wicked are annihilated, not eternally tormented: "снизойдёт от Бога огонь, который уничтожит это воинство и очистит землю... грех и грешники навсегда исчезнут из вселенной" (Мал. 4:1). The church's own synthesis calls this "условное бессмертие" (conditional immortality) — [mosadvent.ru distinctive-doctrines essay](https://www.mosadvent.ru/index.php/adout/faith/item/510-chto-otlichaet-adventistskoe-verouchenie).
- **End times.** Belief 25, *Второе пришествие Христа*: "Второе пришествие Христа — это блаженное упование Церкви... Пришествие Спасителя будет буквальным, личным и одновременным для всего мира" (premillennial) (Тит. 2:13). Belief 28, *Новая Земля*, describes the eternal state.
- **Worship and rites.** Belief 15, *Крещение*: "Крещение совершается погружением в воду" (Рим. 6:1—6). Belief 16, *Вечеря Господня*: "Перед принятием символов совершается служение ногоомовения, установленное нашим Божественным Учителем" (footwashing precedes bread/cup) (1 Кор. 11:23—30).
- **Images and icons.** No dedicated Fundamental Belief or official statement on images/icons was located this session (`TODO: verify`).
- **Holy days, sabbath.** Belief 20, *Суббота*: "Милосердный Творец после шести дней творения мира покоился в седьмой день и установил субботний покой для всех людей как памятник творения. Четвёртая заповедь... требует соблюдения седьмого дня, субботы... от захода до захода солнца" (Быт. 2:1—3; Исх. 20:8—11).
- **Organisation and clergy.** General Conference → Division → Union Conference/Mission → Local Conference/Mission → local church. **[official]** same statistics PDF as §4: Divisions 13; Union Conferences 64; Union Missions 62; Local Conferences 437; Local Missions 336; Ordained Ministers, Active: 22,992. Doctrinal and policy authority rests with the General Conference *in session*, not a standing individual office (§3 preamble; also how the 1985 peace statement below was issued).
- **Distinctive rules — health message.** Belief 22, *Христианское поведение*: "Мы должны воздерживаться от нечистой еды, указанной в Писании [Лев. 11:1—47]. Так как употребление алкогольных напитков, табака, наркотиков и злоупотребление лекарствами вредят нашему организму, то нам от этого также необходимо воздерживаться" (1 Кор. 6:19,20; 10:31). The practice traces historically to Joseph Bates (renounced alcohol c. 1822, meat by 1843); a cited longevity study found "мужчины-адвентисты, придерживающиеся вегетарианской диеты, жили на 9,5 года дольше, чем остальные калифорнийцы." **[official]** [BRI/ESD health-doctrine PDF](https://bri.esd.adventist.org/uploaded_assets/114816).
- **Distinctive rules — historical non-combatancy.** The General Conference's own 1985 peace statement (issued by GC President Neal C. Wilson, June 27, 1985, New Orleans session): "Церковь адвентистов седьмого дня убеждает все народы «перековать мечи свои на орала, и копья свои — на серпы» (Ис. 2:4)." **[official]** [BRI/ESD war-and-peace anthology PDF](https://bri.esd.adventist.org/uploaded_assets/113416). The same anthology clarifies this is a matter of individual conscience, not a blanket prohibition: "официальные документы Церкви АСД не запрещают службу в армии."

### 6. Sensitive points and common misconceptions to avoid

- **"Soul sleep" is a specific theological position, not materialism.** The church's own term is that death is "состояние небытия" until the resurrection (Belief 26) — grounded in specific proof-texts (Eccl. 9:5–6), not a denial of a future bodily resurrection.
- **Ellen G. White's writings are explicitly, officially stated by the church itself not to be Scripture or equal to it** (§3) — this is the church's own position, not an outside critique.
- **"Adventist" is not unique to this church** — many bodies emerged from the 1840s Millerite movement using "Adventist" in their name; the Seventh-day Adventist Church is one specific body, formally organized in 1863. **[official]** [BRI/ESD doctrinal-history PDF](https://bri.esd.adventist.org/uploaded_assets/100451) — "адвентистское движение возникло не в одной какой-то конкретной церкви... не ограничивалось одной страной."
- **The "investigative judgement"** (следственный суд, Belief 24) is presented here strictly as the church's own doctrinal self-description, not as a disputed claim.
- No "How others critique it" line is included for this tradition: no source for an external critique was opened this session.

## The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (Церковь Иисуса Христа Святых последних дней)

### 1. Identity and self-understanding

The church dates its founding to Joseph Smith's "First Vision," traditionally placed in the spring of **1820**, when, as a 14-year-old seeking the true church, he reported that God the Father and Jesus Christ appeared to him in a grove of trees near his home in western New York.

- **[official]** [churchofjesuschrist.org, "First Vision"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/topics/restoration/first-vision) — "God the Father and Jesus Christ appeared to Joseph Smith as he prayed in a grove of trees near his home in western New York."

The organization itself was formally established on **April 6, 1830**, in Fayette, New York. The church frames this as a **"Restoration,"** not the founding of a new denomination: after the apostolic age, "Christ's New Testament Church was lost from the earth," and heavenly messengers (the resurrected John the Baptist, then Peter, James and John) restored baptismal and apostolic priesthood authority to Joseph Smith.

- **[official]** [churchofjesuschrist.org, "A Bicentennial Proclamation to the World"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/the-restoration-of-the-fulness-of-the-gospel-of-jesus-christ/a-bicentennial-proclamation-to-the-world?lang=eng) — "The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, organized on April 6, 1830, is Christ's New Testament Church restored."

### 5. Positions on the comparison topics

- **God (the Godhead).** Three separate, distinct persons/beings united in purpose, not the Nicene one-being-three-persons formula: "there are three separate persons in the Godhead: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost... the Father and the Son have tangible bodies of flesh and bone and... the Holy Ghost is a personage of spirit" (D&C 130:22–23). **[official]** [Bible Dictionary: "God"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/god?lang=eng). "Although the members of the Godhead are distinct beings with distinct roles, they are one in purpose and doctrine." **[official]** [Gospel Topics, "Godhead"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/godhead?lang=eng).
- **The name of God.** "Elohim" generally denotes the Father; "the personage known as Jehovah in Old Testament times... is the Son, known as Jesus Christ." **[official]** Bible Dictionary "God", above. "Jehovah is the premortal Jesus Christ." **[official]** [Bible Dictionary: "Jehovah"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jehovah?lang=eng).
- **Jesus Christ.** Identified with the premortal Jehovah of the Old Testament; "the eldest of the spirit children of Elohim," working "under the direction of the Father." **[official]** Bible Dictionary "God", above.
- **The Holy Ghost.** "A personage of spirit without flesh and bone," third member of the Godhead. **[official]** Bible Dictionary "God" / D&C 130:22–23, above.
- **Scripture and canon.** The four standard works (§2); extensible by revelation — later texts have been formally canonized by common consent (e.g. Official Declarations 1 and 2). **[official]** ["The Role of Prophets"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/prophets-questions?lang=eng). `TODO: verify` — no page using the literal phrase "open canon" was found; this description is inferred from the sourced canonization mechanism, not a direct quotation.
- **Tradition / interpretive authority.** Continuing revelation through the living prophet and Quorum of the Twelve (§3); priesthood authority is described as formally "restored" through heavenly messengers (§1), not continuously transmitted.
- **Salvation.** Both grace and obedience: "Through the grace of God, all can be saved from their sins" via faith, repentance, baptism and the Holy Ghost, then "enduring to the end"; exaltation is to "know Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ and dwell with Them forever." **[official]** [Gospel Topics, "Salvation"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/salvation?lang=eng).
- **Soul, death, afterlife, judgement.** Three post-judgement "kingdoms of glory" (D&C 76): celestial ("dwell forever in the presence of God the Father and His Son Jesus Christ"), terrestrial ("the presence of the Son, but not... the Father"), telestial (for those who "received not the gospel of Christ"). **[official]** [Gospel Topics, "Kingdoms of Glory"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/kingdoms-of-glory?lang=eng).
- **End times.** At the Second Coming, "Christ will reign personally upon the earth" through a literal Millennium during which Satan is "bound." **[official]** [Gospel Topics, "Millennium"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/millennium?lang=eng).
- **Worship and rites.** Weekly Sunday "sacrament meeting" (bread and water; ordinary public meetinghouses). **[official]** [True to the Faith, "Sacrament"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/true-to-the-faith/sacrament?lang=eng). Baptism by immersion at "the age of accountability... eight years of age." **[official]** [True to the Faith, "Baptism"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/true-to-the-faith/baptism?lang=eng). Temple worship (marriage/sealing, endowment, proxy baptism) is explicitly distinct from Sunday worship and restricted to members with a current temple recommend. **[official]** [Temple FAQ](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/temples/faq?lang=eng).
- **Images and icons.** No cross on church buildings: "the Church does not use the symbol of His death as the symbol of our faith... our message is a declaration of the living Christ." **[official]** [Gospel Topics, "Cross"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/cross?lang=eng).
- **Holy days, sabbath.** Sunday worship (above); semi-annual "general conference" (first weekends of April and October), broadcast in ~70 languages. **[official]** [Newsroom, "General Conference"](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/general-conference).
- **Organisation and clergy.** Unpaid local lay clergy: "stake presidents and bishops are... unsalaried." President ("prophet") + two counselors = First Presidency; twelve more apostles = Quorum of the Twelve; local bishop leads a ward, stake president leads a stake. **[official]** [Newsroom, "Organizational Structure"](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/topic/organizational-structure-of-the-church).
- **Distinctive rules.** The **Word of Wisdom** (D&C 89), verbatim in Russian: wine/strong drink «нехорошо и непристойно» (v.5, sacramental wine excepted); «крепкие напитки не для живота» (v.7); «табак ни для тела, ни для живота» (v.8); «горячие напитки ни для тела, ни для живота» (v.9, glossed by the church today as tea and coffee). **[official]** [Учение и Заветы 89](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/89?lang=rus); gloss: **[official]** [True to the Faith, "Word of Wisdom"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/true-to-the-faith/word-of-wisdom?lang=eng). Tithing: "Church members give one-tenth of their income to the Lord through His Church" (D&C 119:3–4). **[official]** [Gospel Topics, "Tithing"](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/tithing?lang=eng).

### 5. Positions on the comparison topics

- **God (oneness, not Trinity).** "Мы поклоняемся единственному истинному и Всемогущему Богу, Создателю, чьё имя — Иегова" (Пс. 83:18); the Trinity is rejected as unscriptural: "Иисус не Всемогущий Бог и... учение о Троице не основано на Священном Писании" (Ин. 14:28). **[official]** [Russian core-beliefs FAQ](https://www.jw.org/ru/свидетели-иеговы/часто-задаваемые-вопросы/верования-свидетелей-иеговы/).
- **The name of God.** Central to identity (see §1 name/name-count data in §3/open items below): "Истинный Бог не безымянный. Его имя — Иегова... Иегова — Всемогущий, единственный истинный Бог." **[official]** [wol.jw.org, "Бог"](https://wol.jw.org/ru/wol/d/r2/lp-u/1200001729).
- **Jesus Christ.** Son of God, not Almighty God, subordinate to the Father: "we do not worship Jesus, as we do not believe that he is Almighty God," citing Jesus's own words "The Father is greater than I am" (Ин. 14:28); ruling as heavenly King since 1914 (Откр. 11:15); his ransom sacrifice is "the key to salvation" (Деян. 4:12). **[official]** [jw.org FAQ, "Do Jehovah's Witnesses Believe in Jesus?"](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/believe-in-jesus/) and [FAQ, "Are Jehovah's Witnesses Christians?"](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/are-jehovahs-witnesses-christians/). Distinctively, Jesus (in his prehuman and post-resurrection heavenly existence) is identified with the archangel Michael: "Jesus has 'an archangel's voice' because he *is* the archangel, Michael" (1 Фес. 4:16). **[official]** [jw.org, "Who Is the Archangel Michael?"](https://www.jw.org/en/bible-teachings/questions/archangel-michael/).
- **The Holy Spirit.** Not a person: "The holy spirit is God's power in action, his active force" (Мих. 3:8; Лк. 1:35); "nowhere does [the Bible] name the holy spirit" as it names Jehovah and Jesus (Ис. 42:8). **[official]** [jw.org, "What Is the Holy Spirit?"](https://www.jw.org/en/bible-teachings/questions/what-is-the-holy-spirit/).
- **Scripture and canon.** 66 books, NWT translation — §2 above.
- **Tradition/interpretive authority.** No single creed; ultimate interpretive authority is the Governing Body via the "faithful and discreet slave" — §3 above.
- **Salvation.** Not exclusive to Jehovah's Witnesses: "Many millions who lived in centuries past and who weren't Jehovah's Witnesses will have an opportunity for salvation... there is going to be a resurrection of both the righteous and the unrighteous" (Деян. 24:15); final judgment belongs to Christ, "It's not our job to judge who will or won't be saved" (Ин. 5:22,27). **[official]** [jw.org FAQ, "Are Jehovah's Witnesses the Only Ones Who Will Be Saved?"](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/who-saved/).
- **Soul, death, afterlife, judgement.** No conscious existence after death, no immortal soul: "Когда человек умирает, он перестаёт существовать" (Пс. 146:4; Еккл. 9:5,10); no eternal torment: "Бог не мучит людей в огненном аду"; a future resurrection is expected for billions (Деян. 24:15). **[official]** [Russian core-beliefs FAQ](https://www.jw.org/ru/свидетели-иеговы/часто-задаваемые-вопросы/верования-свидетелей-иеговы/).
- **End times and the future of the world.** "Мы живём в «последние дни»" (2 Тим. 3:1–5), a period dated from 1914 (see §"Open items" below on 1914/607 BCE); Christ's Kingdom is "a real government in heaven" (Дан. 2:44; Мф. 6:9,10). **[official]** same source.
- **Worship and rites.** Baptism by full immersion, for those who have "repented of his sins," in the name of the Father, Son and holy spirit (Деян. 2:41; Мф. 28:19,20). **[official]** [jw.org, "What Is Baptism?"](https://www.jw.org/en/bible-teachings/questions/what-is-baptism/). The Lord's Evening Meal ("Вечеря Господня"/Memorial) is observed once yearly on Nisan 14; unleavened bread and plain red wine, only the "anointed" (heavenly-hope, 144,000, Откр. 5:9,10) partake, the "great crowd" (earthly hope) attends without partaking (Откр. 7:9,10). **[official]** Russian FAQ, [«Вечеря Господня»](https://www.jw.org/ru/свидетели-иеговы/часто-задаваемые-вопросы/вечеря-господня/); [jw.org, "What Is the Lord's Supper?"](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/lords-supper/).
- **Images and icons.** The cross is rejected: "Иисус умер не на кресте, а на обычном столбе"; images generally rejected as idolatry, "Библия строго повелевает христианам: 'Убегайте от идолопоклонства'" (1 Кор. 10:14). **[official]** Russian FAQ, [«Крест»](https://www.jw.org/ru/свидетели-иеговы/часто-задаваемые-вопросы/вера-в-крест/).
- **Holy days, sabbath, festivals.** Only the annual Memorial is observed (above); Christmas, Easter, birthdays and most traditional holidays are not celebrated as of pagan or unscriptural origin: "Рождество, Пасху и Первое мая... эти праздники были связаны с поклонением другим богам, а не Иегове." **[official]** Russian FAQ, [«Какие праздники не отмечают Свидетели Иеговы?»](https://www.jw.org/ru/свидетели-иеговы/часто-задаваемые-вопросы/свидетели-иеговы-праздники/); on birthdays specifically: "The early Christians did not celebrate birthdays" and considered it "a pagan custom." **[official]** [jw.org FAQ, "Why Don't Jehovah's Witnesses Celebrate Birthdays?"](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/birthdays/).
- **Organisation and clergy.** No paid clergy: "мы не получаем плату за нашу христианскую деятельность" (sense confirmed in English: they "do not get paid for our Christian activities"). Structure: local congregation led by a body of elders; ~20 congregations form a circuit under a circuit overseer; Governing Body at world headquarters (Warwick, NY) provides central direction. **[official]** [jw.org FAQ, "How Are Jehovah's Witnesses' Congregations Organized?"](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/congregations-organized/) — "A body of elders supervises each congregation"; "About 20 congregations form a circuit."
- **Distinctive rules.** *Blood*: no whole-blood transfusion; as of a September 2026 update, the four primary blood components and use of a patient's own blood are matters of individual conscience — see "Open items" below. *Political neutrality*: no voting, no political office, no participation in politics, following Jesus's example of declining political office (Ин. 17:14,16; 18:36). **[official]** [jw.org FAQ, "Why Are Jehovah's Witnesses Politically Neutral?"](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/political-neutrality/). *Military service*: refused, on the basis of Isaiah 2:4 ("beat their swords into plowshares") and Matthew 26:52. **[official]** [jw.org FAQ, "Why Don't Jehovah's Witnesses Go to War?"](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/why-dont-jw-go-to-war/).

### 6. Sensitive points and common misconceptions to avoid

- **Self-description explicitly rejects "sect":** "We feel that we have reestablished the form of Christianity that was practiced in the first century," with "primary allegiance to Jehovah God and Jesus Christ, not to the U.S. government or to any other human government," despite U.S. headquarters. **[official]** [jw.org FAQ, "We're Not a Sect"](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/not-a-sect/).
- **Doctrinal change is presented by the organization itself as progressive clarification, not error-correction in the ordinary sense:** "Когда наше понимание Писаний становится более точным, это отражается на наших взглядах" (Притч. 4:18, "the path of the righteous is like the bright morning light"). **[official]** Russian FAQ, [«Менялись ли взгляды Свидетелей Иеговы?»](https://www.jw.org/ru/свидетели-иеговы/часто-задаваемые-вопросы/изменение-учений-свидетелей-иеговы/) — this specific page's text, fetched directly, does **not** itself name 1914/1925/1975; those dates are documented independently in "Open items" below via other official/reference sources.
- **The 144,000 vs. the "great crowd"** is a structural distinction (heavenly vs. earthly hope) that is easy to garble as an exclusive-salvation claim; see §5 "Salvation" above — salvation is explicitly not limited to the 144,000 or to Jehovah's Witnesses.
- **Historical date expectations (1874, 1914, 1925, 1975)** are documented in "Open items" below using the organization's own historical/current publications, stated as historical fact without evaluative framing.
- Per the task's own rule, no evaluative language ("cult," "false prophecy," etc.) is used in this description; where such critique exists elsewhere, it is not reproduced here as this session did not fetch a specific "how others critique it" source for this tradition — flagged as skipped, not answered.
