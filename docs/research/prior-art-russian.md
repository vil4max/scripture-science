# Prior Art and Source Landscape for "religion-map" (Russian-Language Web)

Research date: 2026-09-25. Scope: whether a Russian-language equivalent of the planned "religion-map" site already exists — a neutral BCE/CE timeline; a lineage tree of monotheistic traditions showing both historical descent and each tradition's own view of its continuity; an equal-treatment topic-by-topic comparison quoting each tradition's own scripture in its own Russian translation with a source link for every claim; and pairwise deep dives — which Russian scripture texts the site could quote and under what licence, and the legal situation around quoting the Russian New World Translation given the 2017 ban on Jehovah's Witnesses in Russia.

**Method.** Every source below was opened directly (WebFetch, or `curl` where exact wording needed confirming against the raw page — WebFetch's own output passes through a summarizing model, so it is not reliable for verbatim quotation on its own). Sources are tagged by tier: **[official]** — a tradition's or government body's own primary document, register, court or ECHR ruling; **[reference]** — an academic, encyclopedic or survey source; **[news/NGO]** — reputable journalism or a monitoring NGO, used only where no official or reference source states the fact. Wikipedia and other aggregators were used only to locate primary sources, which are what is cited. Short quotations are reproduced verbatim, in the source's own language, mainly for official legal/statutory/judicial text (not ordinarily copyrightable subject matter) and for licence or terms-of-use notices, where the exact wording is itself the fact being reported; other copyrighted material (news reporting, editorial or marketing copy) is paraphrased rather than reproduced at length, and quotations are kept short throughout. Unverified items are marked `TODO: verify` with what was tried — nothing below is guessed.

### 1.1 Religious-studies encyclopedias and general reference

- **«Основы религиозных культур и светской этики» (ОРКСЭ)**, the mandatory grade-4/5 school course — [ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Основы_религиозных_культур_и_светской_этики](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Основы_религиозных_культур_и_светской_этики) [reference]. Six selectable modules; only Orthodoxy, Islam, Buddhism and Judaism get a dedicated track, plus one shallow multi-faith module — no Catholicism/Protestant families/Adventists/Mormons/JW as separate tracks. Print/PDF textbooks (publisher «Просвещение»), no public comparison website found. Russian Academy of Sciences reviewers are reported to have called early materials "проповеднический" (missionary-like) rather than comparative. No timeline, tree, matrix or pairwise structure exists in public form. Reuse terms `TODO: verify` (institutional textbook, not checked).

- **«Энциклопедия религий»**, ed. Забияко/Красников/Элбакян, publisher confirmed as **Академический проект / Гаудеамус**, Moscow, 2008 (no source corroborated "РИПОЛ классик"). Full scan: [archive.org/details/2008_20241208](https://archive.org/details/2008_20241208) [reference]; description: [esxatos.com/enciklopediya-religiy](https://esxatos.com/enciklopediya-religiy) [reference]. 1,520 pages, 3,400+ alphabetical entries, deliberately mixed authorship presented as free of any single confessional viewpoint. - **«Мифы народов мира»** (ed. Soviet academic mythology encyclopedia; relevant only as background for the "pagan/other" part of a timeline, not the monotheistic comparison core.

- **HSE (Higher School of Economics)** — a paid continuing-education course "Религии мира" ([addenda.hse.ru/world-religion](https://addenda.hse.ru/world-religion) [reference]) and a seminar series "Религии мира: традиции и тексты" ([hum.hse.ru/proj/religious_studies](https://hum.hse.ru/proj/religious_studies) [reference]). ### 1.2 Confessional (Orthodox) sites covering other faiths

- **azbyka.ru** — Orthodox library/encyclopedia run by the non-commercial fund "Азбука веры". Sections on other confessions: [azbyka.ru/inoslavie](https://azbyka.ru/inoslavie); a large "sects" library including dedicated JW/Adventist/Mormon/Baptist material (e.g. [azbyka.ru/svideteli-iegovy](https://azbyka.ru/svideteli-iegovy)); and comparison tables/diagrams at [azbyka.ru/shemy/1/religiovedenie-i-sravnitelnoe-bogoslovie](https://azbyka.ru/shemy/1/religiovedenie-i-sravnitelnoe-bogoslovie) (e.g. "Ислам и христианство. Сравнительная таблица", "Патриарх и Папа") [official, as the confessional body's own material]. This is the broadest scope match to the project's own tradition list, and the only candidate with an actual comparison-table sub-format and bilateral write-ups. It fails neutrality outright: every author found is Orthodox clergy, and the JW article opens by calling the group a "тоталитарная секта" and uses terms like "лжеучение". Scripture is cited with references, but for JW specifically the site quotes Watchtower/Awake! literature rather than the New World Translation, with no link to a primary JW source. No terms-of-use page was found (`azbyka.ru/pravila-ispolzovaniya-materialov-sajta` → 404); reuse terms `TODO: verify`.

- **azbyka.ru — «Тест: Сравнение религий»** — [azbyka.ru/test/sravnenie-religij](https://azbyka.ru/test/sravnenie-religij) [official]. A 22-question catechetical quiz framing Christianity as the sole religion "founded by God" and Islam in adversarial terms — a concrete illustration of the polemical baseline the project's neutral approach would contrast with.

- **Православная энциклопедия** (pravenc.ru), Moscow Patriarchate's Church-Scientific Center — [pravenc.ru](https://www.pravenc.ru/), article "Инославные" at [pravenc.ru/text/468817.html](https://www.pravenc.ru/text/468817.html) [official]. Large (75+ printed volumes), actively maintained, dense citation apparatus (20+ works cited in that one article). Same authorship problem as azbyka — Orthodox theologians describing other traditions — but more measured in tone. Conventional A–Z format; no timeline/tree/matrix/pairwise structure.

- **Древо (drevo-info.ru)**, reader-contributed Orthodox encyclopedia — [drevo-info.ru/articles/1188](https://drevo-info.ru/articles/1188) [official]. Despite the "tree" name, no visual lineage diagram, timeline or comparison — narrative history plus 1995/2014 denominational statistics only.

- **k-istine.ru**, self-described "миссионерско-апологетический проект" — [k-istine.ru](https://www.k-istine.ru/) [official]. Covers sects, Hinduism, Buddhism, Judaism, Catholicism, Protestantism, the occult; explicitly polemical by its own self-description, same category as azbyka/pravenc.

- **pravmir.ru**, Orthodox news portal — explainer "Основные религии России" ([pravmir.ru/religii-rossii/](https://www.pravmir.ru/religii-rossii/) [official]) is editorially bylined and notably more neutral in tone than azbyka, but a single introductory article with no source links for its scripture mentions and none of the planned structural features.

### 1.4 Wikipedia articles, comparison tables and infographics

No single Russian Wikipedia article combines a timeline, lineage tree, topic comparison and sourced scripture quotes. The closest articles found, all [reference]-tier pointers to their own citations rather than primary sources themselves:

- [Авраамические религии](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Авраамические_религии) — history/definition with some inline scripture citations, no table or diagram.

- [Список христианских конфессий](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Список_христианских_конфессий) — a nested taxonomy of ~20,800 confessions (cited to the "World Christian Encyclopedia"), a useful raw list, not a lineage tree or comparison.

- [Различия между православием и католицизмом](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Различия_между_православием_и_католицизмом) — the only genuine pairwise comparison found on ru.wiki (Filioque, Mariology, papal authority, sacraments, purgatory, calendar, icons); narrative rather than tabular, mixed tone, minimal scripture sourcing.

- [Сравнительное религиоведение](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Сравнительное_религиоведение) — about the academic discipline itself, not a comparison resource.

- [Численность последователей основных религий](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Численность_последователей_основных_религий) — well-sourced (Pew/Britannica/Melton) demographic tables only, no doctrine.

### 1.5 A standalone "map/picture of world religions" project, under any name

Targeted searches (`карта религий мира сайт`, `сравнение религий проект`, `генеалогическое древо религий сайт`, `атлас религий мира`, `религиозная картина мира проект`, and variants) found **no standalone Russian-language website or app whose whole purpose is a neutral, sourced, multi-tradition religious comparison** — every promising lead resolved into one of the categories above (a single encyclopedia article, a demographic map, confessional apologetics, or an academic project with no public deliverable). This is a confirmed negative, not an absence of search effort.

### 1.6 Open-source repositories (not Russian-language, but relevant to reuse/contribute)

No Russian-language open-source project was found on GitHub or GitLab (a GitLab search returned nothing relevant at all). Two English-language repositories are conceptually adjacent and worth noting for the build-vs-reuse decision:

- **SkipRoads01/christianity** — [github.com/SkipRoads01/christianity](https://github.com/SkipRoads01/christianity) [reference] — a static site with a branching SVG timeline (AD 30–present) and an 11-tradition × 6-topic comparison table. Structurally close to parts of the project, but has no citations anywhere, no stated licence, and 0 stars/forks.

- **jebboone/deitydb** — [github.com/jebboone/deitydb](https://github.com/jebboone/deitydb) [reference] — a "source-traceable comparative religion ontology" (4,130 entities, claims zero unsourced relationships, MIT licence). Closest in *sourcing philosophy* to "proof for every claim," but scoped to ancient/mythological pantheons, not living monotheistic doctrine, and has no Russian content.

### 1.7 Parallel-text scripture sites (infrastructure, not comparison projects)

Every site below is, by its own homepage/about page, a single-tradition scripture host, not a cross-religion comparison project — relevant here as potential linkable infrastructure for the "verse texts" requirement (full licensing detail is in §3):

- **[azbyka.ru/biblia](https://azbyka.ru/biblia)** [official]. Translations: Synodal, Church Slavonic, Kassian, Yungerov, Porfiry, Averintsev and others; 40+ languages in total. Parallel view: no — a single-translation switcher only. Per-verse permalinks: yes (`?Jn.3:16`-style query string).

Parallel view: **yes** — a genuine two-column comparison, e.g. [bible.by/parallel/syn/nrt/52/1/](https://bible.by/parallel/syn/nrt/52/1/). - **[only.bible](https://only.bible)** (formerly bibleonline.ru) [official]. Translations: Synodal (public domain), KJV (public domain), and several Biblica/IBS translations (© Biblica Inc., "personal use" only per its own copyright page). Parallel view: not confirmed. Per-verse permalinks: yes (`/bible/{code}/{book}-{chapter}.{verse}/`).

- **[bible.com/ru](https://bible.com/ru/bible)** (YouVersion) [official]. Translations: SYNO, NRT, RSP, BTI and others. Parallel view: **yes** — a dedicated `/ru/bible/compare/` tool. Per-verse permalinks: yes.

- **[allbible.info](https://allbible.info)** [official]. Translations: at least 4 of a stated 6 confirmed (Synodal, ASV, KJV, Modern). - **[quran-online.ru](https://quran-online.ru)** [official]. Translations: Кулиев, Абу Адель, and the ас-Саади tafsir; **not** Крачковский, Османов, Порохова or Саблуков. Parallel view: de facto — all available renderings are stacked on one ayah page. Per-ayah permalinks: yes (`/{surah}:{ayah}`).

- **[ru-koran.com](https://ru-koran.com)** [official]. Translations: Кулиев, Крачковский, Османов, Порохова, Саблуков plus 3 others; no Абу Адель. - **[ru.chabad.org](https://ru.chabad.org/library/bible_cdo/aid/5764227/jewish/Глава-1.htm)** [official]. Translation: "Тора с комментарием Раши" (publisher «Книжники»); **not** confirmed as the Сончино edition. Parallel view: no (Hebrew/Russian/commentary together, not multiple translations). Per-verse permalinks: verses are numbered within chapter pages; no distinct per-verse URL was confirmed.

## 2. The five closest matches, ranked

**1. azbyka.ru — «Инославие»/«Секты» sections and comparison «Схемы».** [azbyka.ru/inoslavie](https://azbyka.ru/inoslavie), [azbyka.ru/shemy/1/religiovedenie-i-sravnitelnoe-bogoslovie](https://azbyka.ru/shemy/1/religiovedenie-i-sravnitelnoe-bogoslovie) — Structurally and topically the closest of everything found: it covers exactly the planned tradition list (Orthodoxy's relationship to Catholicism, Protestant families, Adventists, Mormons and Jehovah's Witnesses), and already publishes comparison tables and bilateral write-ups resembling the project's pairwise format. That is also exactly why it cannot be reused or extended: every article is written by Orthodox clergy about other traditions, in openly polemical language for the less mainstream ones, and no tradition is quoted describing itself in its own translation. It is the clearest illustration of the gap the project would fill — same subject matter, opposite epistemics.

**2. «Энциклопедия религий» (Академический проект / Гаудеамус, 2008).** [archive.org/details/2008_20241208](https://archive.org/details/2008_20241208) — The best match on editorial philosophy: a large (3,400+ entry) reference work with deliberately mixed, non-confessional authorship and an explicit anti-bias editorial stance, covering the full range of world religions including every tradition the project plans to feature. It is, however, a conventional alphabetical encyclopedia with no timeline, no lineage tree, no topic-by-topic matrix, no pairwise deep dives, and no per-claim linking to primary scripture — useful as a citable secondary source during content research, not as a structural precedent.

**3. Arzamas — «Сравнительная таблица главных христианских течений».** [arzamas.academy/materials/1265](https://arzamas.academy/materials/1265) — The best match on format: an actual neutral, tabular, topic-by-topic comparison, credited to a named compiler and citing real scholarship. It proves that a genuinely neutral comparison table is achievable in Russian in this space. Its scope is narrow (five Christian traditions only, no Judaism, Islam, Adventists, Mormons or Jehovah's Witnesses), it cites scholarly literature rather than each tradition's own scripture per claim, and it has no accompanying timeline, lineage tree or pairwise deep-dive structure.

**4. Православная энциклопедия (pravenc.ru).** [pravenc.ru/text/468817.html](https://www.pravenc.ru/text/468817.html) — The best match on scale and citation discipline: a 75-plus-volume, actively maintained academic-institutional project with dense sourcing (20-plus works cited in a single article). It shares azbyka's core problem — one tradition's scholars writing about all the others — in a more measured register, and it is a conventional encyclopedia, not a comparison tool.

**5. «Атлас религий и национальностей России» (sreda.org/arena).** [sreda.org/arena](https://sreda.org/arena) — The best match on production quality: professionally designed interactive maps, infographics and open datasets, built on a large real survey. It explicitly excludes doctrinal/theological content by design, so it has no overlap with the comparison core of the project — it would only ever be a statistics companion, never a competitor or a reusable base.

## 3. Scripture texts and licences

**Quotation exception — ст. 1274 ГК РФ**, confirmed via [consultant.ru](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/84bbd636598a59112a4fe972432343dd4f51da1d/) [official]: quotation of lawfully published works is free (no consent or payment required) for scientific, polemical, critical, informational or educational purposes, "в объёме, оправданном целью цитирования" ("to the extent justified by the purpose of the quotation"), on condition that the author's name and the source are indicated. This is the provision a comparison site quoting short, attributed, source-linked passages would rely on for texts that are still under copyright.

**Term of protection — ст. 1281 ГК РФ**, confirmed via [consultant.ru](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/675b81f0bc9b8ecd68ea332ecfd9d1562541fb98/) and [rulaws.ru](https://rulaws.ru/gk-rf-chast-4/Razdel-VII/Glava-70/Statya-1281/) [official]: exclusive rights last for the author's life plus 70 years from 1 January of the year after death, with a special rule for works first published posthumously — in that case the 70 years run from publication, not death, "при условии, что произведение было обнародовано в течение семидесяти лет после смерти автора" (п.3). This posthumous-publication rule is decisive for Krachkovsky's Quran translation below.

**Bible translations**

- **Синодальный перевод (1876)** — **public domain**. Basis: an institutional/Synod translation, roughly 150 years old. Full text: [bible.by/syn/1/1/](https://bible.by/syn/1/1/), which itself states "Public Domain — общественное достояние" [official]. Per-verse links: yes.

- **Церковнославянская / Елизаветинская Библия (1751)** — **public domain**. Basis: an institutional translation, roughly 275 years old. Full text: [bible.by/elzs/1/1/](https://bible.by/elzs/1/1/), the same "Public Domain" statement [official]. Per-verse links: yes.

Per-verse links: structurally yes on wol.jw.org, but jw.org's Terms of Use restrict re-posting the text elsewhere (see notes below and §4).

## 4. Legal note on Jehovah's Witnesses materials in Russia

*Factual summary only — not legal advice. Facts are attributed to their sources; characterizations belong to the named source, not to this document.*

**The 2017 liquidation**

- On 20 April 2017 the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation (case № АКПИ17-238) granted the Ministry of Justice's claim and ordered the liquidation of "Управленческий центр Свидетелей Иеговы в России" together with its 395 local religious organisations, with remaining property passing to the state.

- Source, cross-verified word-for-word between a hosted scan of the decision ([globalfreedomofexpression.columbia.edu](https://globalfreedomofexpression.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/Jehovah.pdf), p. 39) and a mirror at [legalacts.ru](https://legalacts.ru/sud/reshenie-verkhovnogo-suda-rf-ot-20042017-n-akpi17-238/) [official]: "Ликвидировать Религиозную организацию «Управленческий центр Свидетелей Иеговы в России»... Обратить имущество... в собственность Российской Федерации."

- An appeal was rejected on 17 July 2017 (№ АПЛ17-216) — independently confirmed on the Supreme Court's own case tracker [vsrf.ru](https://www.vsrf.ru/lk/practice/claims/5411514) and on Minjust's own register [minjust.gov.ru/ru/documents/7822/](https://minjust.gov.ru/ru/documents/7822/), both [official], both dated 17.07.2017.

- An earlier apparent "17 мая 2017 / № ПКП17-216" reading from one secondary-page fetch could not be corroborated anywhere and is treated as an isolated fetch error, not a real second ruling.

**Federal List of Extremist Materials — the New World Translation**

- Following a Vyborg City Court (Leningrad Region) decision of 17 August 2017, the Russian New World Translation was added, with three other Jehovah's Witnesses publications, to the Федеральный список экстремистских материалов — [sova-center.ru](https://www.sova-center.ru/misuse/news/persecution/2017/08/d37666/) [news/NGO] (the court decision itself was not independently located; `TODO: verify` its case number — only an unverified "2-5/2017" was seen).

- Minjust's own register confirms the entry directly: [minjust.gov.ru/ru/extremist-materials/?page=45](https://minjust.gov.ru/ru/extremist-materials/?page=45) [official], entry **No. 4488**, describing a specific printing — "Made in the United States of America," dated March 2014, 1,787 pages — citing "Выборгского городского суда Ленинградской области от 17.08.2017".

- Russian Wikipedia separately states the 2007 edition was the one listed, which conflicts with Minjust's "March 2014" description. The primary Minjust entry is weighted as authoritative here, but `TODO: verify` exactly which printing(s) are covered if a specific edition matters for the site.

**Ongoing enforcement**

- Individual believers continue to be prosecuted under Criminal Code Art. 282.2 (organising or participating in a banned extremist organisation's activity) for religious activity carried out after the 2017 ban.

- A Krasnodar Krai believer received a 2-year suspended sentence on 8 July 2026, reported as the 16th such conviction in that locality — [sova-center.ru](https://www.sova-center.ru/misuse/news/persecution/2026/07/d50638/) [news/NGO].

- Six believers were sentenced in Moscow on 26 August 2026, to terms ranging from 3 years 8 months to 5 years — [sova-center.ru](https://www.sova-center.ru/misuse/news/persecution/2026/08/d54430/) [news/NGO].

**Access to jw.org from Russia**

- jw.org states its own site was added to the Federal List of Extremist Materials on 21 July 2015 and has since been blocked by Russian ISPs, with promoting it from within Russia a criminal offence — [jw.org's own account](https://www.jw.org/en/news/region/russia/bans-jw-org-website-blocked/) [official].

- Independent sources report earlier and related procedural dates within the same overall blocking process — an initial prosecutors' order of 7 August 2013, a Supreme Court recognition of 2 December 2014, and an appellate confirmation of 16 January 2015 — [Forum 18](https://www.forum18.org/archive.php?article_id=2935), 4 October 2024 [news/NGO] — without any one source tying the full sequence together.

- The Russia-specific domain jw-russia.org was blocked separately, via a Kuibyshev District Court (Omsk) decision of 19 March 2018 — [sova-center.ru](https://www.sova-center.ru/misuse/news/persecution/2018/07/d39763/) [news/NGO].

- Forum 18's October 2024 reporting is the most recent confirmation found that the block remains in effect. The Roskomsvoboda blocklist registry (reestr.rublacklist.net) could not be reached in this session (repeated HTTP 500 errors) to check current status — `TODO: verify`.

### Grounds of the 2017 ban and labelling

**1. Precise legal grounds**

- The Ministry of Justice's claim, as recited in the Supreme Court's own decision, cited **Federal Law No. 114-FZ «О противодействии экстремистской деятельности»**, часть 4 статьи 7 and статья 9, together with **Federal Law No. 125-FZ «О свободе совести и о религиозных объединениях»**, пункт 4 статьи 6 and пункт 7 статьи 14 — [globalfreedomofexpression.columbia.edu](https://globalfreedomofexpression.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/Jehovah.pdf), p. 3 [official].

- Law 114-FZ Art. 1 defines "экстремистская деятельность" to include incitement of religious hatred and propaganda of the "exclusivity, superiority or inferiority" of a person by religious affiliation, among other grounds — [base.garant.ru](https://base.garant.ru/12127578/1cafb24d049dcd1e7707a22d98e9858f/) [official].

- Art. 9 of the same law provides that a religious association found to have engaged in extremist activity may be liquidated by court order on Minjust's application, with its regional and other structural subdivisions liquidated along with it — [base.garant.ru](https://base.garant.ru/12127578/493aff9450b0b89b29b367693300b74a/) [official]. This is the specific clause under which the 395 local organisations were liquidated together with the Administrative Center.

- Minjust's own register of organisations found extremist lists the Administrative Center as entry No. 62, citing both court decisions and "часть 4 статьи 9" — [minjust.gov.ru/ru/documents/7822/](https://minjust.gov.ru/ru/documents/7822/) [official].

- On the faith-versus-organisation distinction: the decision's reasoning consistently addresses a **named registered legal entity's conduct** — "Установление ответственности религиозной организации за осуществление экстремистской деятельности не может рассматриваться как нарушение конституционного права на объединение" ("holding a religious organisation liable for extremist activity cannot be regarded as violating the constitutional right of association") — rather than containing any affirmative statement that the ruling leaves the underlying faith untouched. The distinction is implicit in the organisation-and-conduct framing, not an express disclaimer — same source [official].

**2. Balance**

- Jehovah's Witnesses' own position, via jw.org (an English statement and a Russian-language version at [jw.org/ru](https://www.jw.org/ru/новости/регионы/россия/реакция-международной-общественности-на-решение-верховного-суда/) [official]): the organisation calls the ban unjust and states its intention to pursue the matter at the European Court of Human Rights.

- The ECHR has since ruled, in ***Taganrog LRO and Others v. Russia*** (applications no. 32401/10 and 19 others, judgment 7 June 2022, final 7 September 2022) — [hudoc.echr.coe.int/eng?i=001-217535](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/eng?i=001-217535) [official] — that Russia had violated, variously by unanimous or six-votes-to-one decisions: Article 10 (freedom of expression), over the designation of JW publications as extremist, which the Court found was not "prescribed by law"; Article 9 read with Article 11, over the dissolution of individual local organisations and, separately, over the dissolution of the Administrative Center together with all local organisations; Article 9 again, over the criminal prosecution of individual believers; Article 5, for one applicant's detention; and Article 1 of Protocol No. 1, over the confiscation of assets.

- The Court declined to rule separately on the applicants' Article 14 (discrimination) complaint, holding it unnecessary given the Article 9/11 findings — so this is not itself a finding of discrimination.

- On the Administrative Center's dissolution specifically, the Court's own reasoning (§254) called it part of "a policy of intolerance by the Russian authorities... designed to cause [them] to abandon their faith" — directly contrasting with the Russian court's own organisation-conduct framing above.

- Russia ceased to be a party to the European Convention on Human Rights on 16 September 2022 — the Court's own resolution [echr.coe.int](https://www.echr.coe.int/documents/d/echr/Resolution_ECHR_cessation_Russia_Convention_20220916_ENG) [official]: "the Russian Federation ceases to be a High Contracting Party to the European Convention on Human Rights... on 16 September 2022" — while remaining competent over acts/omissions before that date. The domestic enforceability of this judgment inside Russia today is a separate, contested matter, beyond this note's scope.

**3. Labelling requirement**

- Статья 4 of the Law «О средствах массовой информации» prohibits distributing information about an organisation on the published list of those recognised as extremist unless it is noted that «соответствующая организация ликвидирована либо ее деятельность запрещена» ("the organisation concerned has been liquidated or its activity banned") — [base.garant.ru](https://base.garant.ru/10164247/1b93c134b90c6071b4dc3f495464b753/) [official].

- КоАП РФ ст. 13.15 ч. 2 makes the same omission an administrative offence, currently fined 2,000–2,500 rubles for individuals and 40,000–50,000 for legal entities, with confiscation — [base.garant.ru](https://base.garant.ru/12125267/07a4a413953ad94308be69165d05fd74/) [official].

- **Whether this reaches an ordinary website that is not a registered mass medium is not clearly settled by any primary source found.** Other paragraphs of Art. 4 explicitly extend to "информационно-телекоммуникационные сети" (any online network); the extremist-organisation clause does not use that phrase. КоАП 13.15 ч. 2's own text is not limited to media entities and separately penalises private citizens.

- A Supreme Court Plenum resolution confirms only the general principle that non-registered websites are not exempt from liability generally, without naming this specific provision — [base.garant.ru/1795394/](https://base.garant.ru/1795394/) [official].

- The one concrete enforcement example located was against a registered online publication, not an arbitrary unregistered site — [10.rkn.gov.ru via web.archive.org](https://web.archive.org/web/2023/https://10.rkn.gov.ru/news/news342051.htm) [official], which also states outlets choose their own notice wording (no single mandated formula).

- A commonly used real example, from a mainstream outlet: *«Свидетели Иеговы» (организация признана экстремистской и запрещена в России)* — [lenta.ru, 31 Aug 2026](https://lenta.ru/news/2026/08/31/raskryt-sostav/) [news/NGO].

**4. КоАП ст. 20.29 (mass distribution of listed extremist materials)**

- This article penalises "массовое распространение экстремистских материалов, включенных в... список..., либо их производство или хранение в целях массового распространения" ("mass distribution of extremist materials on the... list..., or their production or storage for the purpose of mass distribution") — fines from 1,000 rubles up to 15 days' administrative arrest for individuals, higher for officials and legal entities, with confiscation — [consultant.ru](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_34661/36f65566525347506c3bca47d7b5ddfc19731d6b/) [official].

- The operative trigger is explicitly "массовое" (mass) distribution, or production/storage "for the purpose of" mass distribution; the article yields to the Criminal Code and to several other named KoAP articles, and is due to be amended from 3 November 2026 (Federal Law No. 294-FZ), not examined here.

- Since the New World Translation is itself on the Federal List of Extremist Materials (entry No. 4488, above), this article's general scope — not any conclusion about a specific website — is a relevant fact for anyone considering publishing its text online.

- SOVA-Center reports several past applications of this article to individual social-media posts, e.g. a 1,000-ruble fine over a VKontakte video — [sova-center.ru via web.archive.org](https://web.archive.org/web/2026/https://www.sova-center.ru/misuse/news/persecution/2023/02/d47693/) [news/NGO].

## 5. Conclusion

**Does something already cover this concept in Russian? No.** No Russian-language resource found combines a neutral BCE/CE timeline, a dual (historical-and-self-described) lineage tree, an equal-treatment topic-by-topic comparison matrix sourced to each tradition's own scripture, and pairwise deep dives, in one project. The candidates split into three non-overlapping groups, none of them a competitor:

- (a) Genuinely neutral, multi-authored academic references — «Энциклопедия религий», Arzamas's comparison table — that get the *epistemics* right but not the *structure* (no timeline/tree/matrix/pairwise format, no per-claim scripture linking).

- (b) Structurally closer, confessionally one-sided Orthodox sites — azbyka.ru, pravenc.ru, k-istine.ru — that get parts of the *structure* right (comparison tables, bilateral write-ups, the exact tradition list including JW/Adventists/Mormons) but fail neutrality by design, since every description is written by one tradition's theologians about the others.

- (c) Demographic/statistical projects — ARENA/sreda.org — with strong production values and zero doctrinal content.

A dedicated search for a same-concept project under any name, and for an open-source codebase to fork or contribute to, both came back empty; the closest reusable code (SkipRoads01/christianity, jebboone/deitydb) is English-language, uncited or narrowly scoped, and not a serious head start.

**What would be genuinely new:**

1. The combination itself — no existing project attempts all four planned parts together.

2. Treating Jehovah's Witnesses, Mormons and Seventh-day Adventists on genuinely equal footing with Orthodoxy and Catholicism, each quoting its own scripture in its own translation rather than being described by another tradition's apologists — every Russian-language resource with this tradition list (azbyka, pravenc, k-istine) is written from inside one tradition looking out.

3. A per-claim, per-verse sourcing discipline (a link for every quote, number and date) — approximated in spirit only by an English-language mythology-ontology side project (DeityDB), never found in a Russian doctrinal-comparison context.

4. A dual lineage tree separating historical/scholarly descent from each tradition's own claimed continuity — not found anywhere in this research, Russian or otherwise.

5. Explicit, sourced awareness of the licensing and legal landscape around the specific texts to be quoted: the Russian New World Translation is itself on the Federal List of Extremist Materials, quoting it intersects with the labelling duties of Art. 4 of the Mass Media Law/КоАП 13.15 and the general scope of КоАП 20.29 in ways no primary source resolves cleanly for a non-media website (§4), and most modern translations in the comparison set (recent Quran and Tanakh translations, the NWT, the Book of Mormon) remain under copyright with only ст. 1274's quotation exception, not free republication, available (§3).

No existing Russian project appears to have navigated this combination of editorial and legal questions in public. Building it new, rather than reusing or contributing to existing work, is the path this research supports — with the Synodal and Church Slavonic texts, the public-domain Sablukov Quran, and short, attributed, source-linked quotations from everything else as the realistic starting inventory of quotable primary text.
